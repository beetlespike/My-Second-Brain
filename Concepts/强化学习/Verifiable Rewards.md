# Verifiable Rewards

把完整回答交给明确的判定规则，例如检查最终数值。采用相同步骤判定时，不同推理过程也可能得到相同奖励。

A checker scores a finished response, for example by comparing its last answer with a specified target. Distinct reasoning traces can receive the same outcome reward. [Group Sampling](Group%20Sampling.md) reveals how often the verifier distinguishes outcomes for a given prompt.
